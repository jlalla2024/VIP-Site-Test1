Our Research

Our research explores how smart technologies, such as sensors and artificial intelligence (AI), can help balance the needs of tourists and residents. By studying local conditions and human behavior, we aim to design solutions protecting coastal areas while supporting a thriving tourism economy.

Our Strategy

Community-centered AI and Data Science to co-design resilient coastal destinations for everyone

  • Gather information from environmental sensors, live video feeds, and social media
  • Conduct surveys and focus groups with residents and tourists
  • Use AI and data science to find patterns in behaviors and stress points (e.g., overcrowding, waste hotspots, resident-visitor conflicts)
  • Build interactive dashboards for transparent, community-friendly decision making

Social Values and Impact

Our project helps Florida’s coasts remain vibrant and healthy for future generations.

  • Help residents enjoy a better quality of life with reduced stress and congestion
  • Offer tourists safer, cleaner, and more enjoyable experiences
  • Support policymakers and local businesses with data-driven tools for smarter planning

Preliminary proof of concept

Beach occupancy monitoring

Using public beach-camera images, this project explores how computer vision can track beach occupancy and help communities understand crowding.

The early prototype captures images and logs estimated visitor counts over time.

Bao Nguyen · Dr. Soyoung Park · Dr. Jinwoo Jang

Method and next steps

In one initial test frame, the prototype detected 12 of 19 people present. This preliminary result does not establish performance across all conditions. The pipeline uses OpenCV for frame capture and YOLOv8 for object detection. Planned work includes comparing density-map models with object detection, evaluating occlusion, and expanding to multiple camera feeds.

The research poster includes the data sources, references and U.S. Department of Education funding acknowledgment, Grant No. P116H230018.
